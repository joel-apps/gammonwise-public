Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what the GammonWise app for iPhone and iPad does with your data. GammonWise is published by Joel Hernandez (“we”). Contact: joelhcode@gmail.com.

In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. Your progress stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. We collect a few anonymous usage statistics to improve the app.

Your progress

Your answers, streak, rating, mistake log, review schedule, today’s set, the level you chose, the lessons you’ve read, and whether you finished the welcome screens and saw the one-time offer are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in your own iCloud account (Apple’s iCloud key-value storage) so that your progress follows you to your other iPhone or iPad. That copy is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we cannot see it. Settings such as language, reminders and sounds stay on each device.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used. Only the App Store version of the app sends statistics, and only these events: onboarding started and completed, and the level you chose (beginner, club or strong); each answered drill (its chapter, which drill, and whether the answer was right, close or wrong); when the purchase screen is shown and where from; when a purchase is started or completed, its plan (lifetime or yearly), where it was started and whether Pro unlocked; restores and where from; and when you share a position (its chapter). The app does not send automatic events such as app opens. The events include technical details: the app’s name, version and build, the device type and model, screen size, iOS version, language and time zone. They are linked to random identifiers created by the app (one for the install, one for each session), not to your name, email or Apple ID. When the app starts it also asks PostHog for its settings; that request carries no identifier and none of your usage data. PostHog stores these events in the United States. The app turns off PostHog’s location lookup, and we configure PostHog not to keep IP addresses. We use this data only to improve the app.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases are active. RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier.

Reminders

The daily reminder is off until you switch it on. It is scheduled on your device, and no notification data is sent to us.

Sharing a position

When you tap Share this position, the app draws an image of the position on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The image contains only the position, the game situation (money game or match score, and which side is yours), its question and answer, and the app’s name and tagline. It goes only where you choose to send it. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add it to your photos; the app cannot read your photos.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your data, except with the service providers named above for the purposes described.

Retention and your choices

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. The iCloud copy stays in your iCloud account, where only you and Apple can reach it. Usage statistics are kept for up to one year. Because they are anonymous we usually cannot link them to you, but you can email us with any request about your data and we will help as far as we can.

Children

GammonWise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
