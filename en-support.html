GammonWise

A backgammon trainer: a 12-chapter course from pip counting to match play, a daily set of positions, and endless practice. Every answer is checked by computer rollouts or exact calculation.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Email joelhcode@gmail.com. If something isn’t working, please include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Common questions

How are the answers worked out?

Ahead of time on our computers (not on your phone), with the free GNU Backgammon program: thousands of games played out from each position (rollouts), or an exact calculation where one exists. The app ships only the results, so it works offline.

Does my progress sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, through your own iCloud account, when you are signed in to iCloud on both devices. There is no GammonWise account. Settings such as language, reminders and sounds stay on each device.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings in the app (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → GammonWise and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment with nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn the daily reminder or the sounds on or off?

In the app’s Settings. The reminder is off until you switch it on, and you can pick its time. Sounds follow your device’s silent switch.

Is this gambling?

No. GammonWise is a training app for the board game. There is no betting, no real money and no play against other people.
